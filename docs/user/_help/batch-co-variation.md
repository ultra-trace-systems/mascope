**Co-variation** is how a compound's peaks follow each other across the batch where
the batch sees it through two ionization channels or more - protonated and
ammoniated, deprotonated and clustered with the reagent. *Read co-variation* reads
every such pair: the log intensities, divided by each spectrum's reagent signal, with
what all of the batch's peaks share taken out, and correlated over the spectra both
peaks are in. At **r 0.6** or more the two co-vary; below they do not; a pair held in
fewer than ten spectra together, or with a peak that hardly moves, is
**untestable**. The column shows the strongest partner's r. The reading is shown,
never weighed: no tier moves on it, and a pair running against each other demotes
nothing.
