The **Why** box is titled for the row's tier. Its bar draws the evidence
against the run's own bands, marked where *candidate* and *assigned* begin, and
the product line under it gives the fit and plausibility the evidence is made
of. Then one line per reason the run found, with the number or formula it turned
on; click a reason for the run's own sentence. The evidence sets the highest tier
a row can reach, and a row short of the top band says so first. Every rule after
it can only lower the tier: each names what capped the row at *candidate* &mdash;
a radical neutral, rivals the evidence left standing, an ion another neutral
explains as well through another channel, a line a neighbour's isotope pattern
already predicts, a line of its own pattern the spectrum should show and does
not, a peak the instrument did not separate from its neighbour &mdash; or what
the row kept its tier on. What holds the tier back comes first, in the warning
colour; what supports it follows, in the support colour. A second channel or a
series lists the batch's rows it spans, each m/z a link to its peak. An
isotopologue lists its M0's reasons beneath its own. Where a reason names a
neighbouring peak, **neighbour at m/z** beside it focuses that peak.

**Same ion, read another way** lists the other neutrals this ion reads as
through the run's other channels. No mass or isotope pattern tells those
readings apart; a second channel can, and so can the sample showing one of
the molecules far more strongly than the other.

A row assigned by hand lists one reason at most, *expected line absent*, since
the spectrum holds the same lines whoever chose the formula. A run imported from
another engine and a run made before Mascope recorded reasons list none.
