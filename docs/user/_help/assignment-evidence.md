Four numbers describe the evidence behind an assignment. The **fit score**
measures one thing only: how well the observed peaks fit the candidate's
predicted isotope pattern &mdash; the m/z and abundance errors say how far the
measurement lies from that prediction. **Plausibility** grades whether the
formula is chemically sensible at all. Their product is the candidate's
evidence, and **confidence** is the winner's share of it among all candidates
for the peak &mdash; with genuine ties reported as ties rather than resolved by
decree. Finally, **P(correct)** calibrates the evidence into an actual
probability of being correct, per instrument.
