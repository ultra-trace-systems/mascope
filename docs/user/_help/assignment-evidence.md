Four tiles measure how well the formula explains the peak, each a value with a
caption in words:

- **mass error** &mdash; measured minus predicted m/z, in ppm, and under it how
  many widths of the run's measurement model it sits from the model's centre:
  the centre and width the fit was read at too.
- **isotope lines** &mdash; the lines of the predicted isotope pattern found, of
  those predicted, with how far their abundances are from the prediction.
- **confidence** &mdash; the formula's share of the evidence among all
  candidates for the peak. A near-equal runner-up is written out as a *tie*
  rather than resolved by decree.
- **batch** &mdash; the samples the peak's batch peak is in, and how many of the
  batch's readings the card lists for it.

The **fit** (how well the peak and its isotope lines match the prediction: a
pure measurement) and the **plausibility** (whether the formula is chemically
sensible) are in the Why box above, as the product line: fit &times;
plausibility is the **evidence**, the number the tier is read off and the one
the candidates for the peak competed on.
