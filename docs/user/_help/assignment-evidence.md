The evidence grid measures how well the formula explains the peak:

- **fit** &mdash; how well the peak and its isotope lines match the formula's
  predicted isotope pattern: a pure measurement. **m/z error** and **abund.
  error** say how far the measurement lies from the prediction.
- **mass z** &mdash; the m/z error counted in widths of the run's own mass
  calibration at that m/z.
- **plausibility** &mdash; whether the formula is chemically sensible.
- **evidence** &mdash; fit &times; plausibility: the number the tier is read off,
  and the one the candidates for the peak competed on.
- **confidence** &mdash; the formula's share of the evidence among all
  candidates for the peak. A near-equal runner-up is reported as a *tie* rather
  than resolved by decree.
- **P(correct)** &mdash; the evidence calibrated into a probability of being
  correct, per instrument.
