Any assignment can be verified by hand: **confirm** it, **reject** it, or mark
it **unsure**, together with the level of evidence behind the judgment &mdash;
from a reference standard, MS/MS or an orthogonal technique down to the
isotope/adduct pattern or a visual check. Confirming requires picking an
evidence level, and rejecting is a first-class answer, not a lesser one:
confirmed and rejected assignments together form the labelled record that
future confidence calibration is fit on. Verdicts attach to the peak, formula
and ionization mechanism rather than to one run, so they carry over when the
sample is re-assigned. One verdict covers a whole compound: judging it from any
of its isotopologue peaks records a single verdict, shown on all of them.
