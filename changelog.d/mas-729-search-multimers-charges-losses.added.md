- **Peak assignment and the composition search read dimers, doubly charged ions
  and neutral losses.** A mechanism such as `[2M+H]+`, `[M+2H]2+`, `[M+Ca]2+` or
  `[M+H-H2O]+` on an ionization mode used to get target ions only; a run left it
  out and logged it, and the composition search refused it. Both now search it at
  its own mass: the molecule's mass is (|z| × m/z − shift) / n, the ion's
  formula is the molecules with the species and less the loss, and the isotope
  lines of a doubly charged ion are predicted half a mass unit apart. A
  mechanism that only removes reads its one H as the proton and the rest as the
  neutral loss (`[M-H-H2O]-` removes a proton and loses water); its spelling and
  the mass it is searched at do not change. Like any loss, it no longer reads a
  molecule that lacks what it loses: `[M-H-H2O]-` and `[M-CO2-H]-` read no
  formula without the oxygen, where they used to.
