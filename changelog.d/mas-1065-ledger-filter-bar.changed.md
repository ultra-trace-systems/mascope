- **The assignment ledgers have a filter bar, and remember it.** A search box
  above both the *Assignments* and the *Batch peaks* ledger finds a formula, a
  listed name or an m/z: within the run's own mass gate in a sample's ledger,
  within 5 ppm in the batch's. A thin tier bar draws each tier's share over
  the tier chips; a tier with no rows has no chip, and the reagent and
  artifact chips sit apart. **Show** replaces the cog: isotopologue lines,
  roles, stripped rows, the batch's flags with one badge kind at a time,
  engine readings, the verdict filter, now in the batch's ledger too, and
  **Group by**. **Export ledger (CSV)** and **Write listed names in full** are
  under **...**. Every choice, the search and the chips included, is kept per
  ledger through a switch between the sample and the batch and a reload;
  before, all but the listing switch reset on every switch. The batch's
  Formula column loses its filter menu to the search.
