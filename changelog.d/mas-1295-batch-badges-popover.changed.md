- **Across the batch's "+n" opens the row's badges.** In the **Batch peaks**
  ledger, a click on **+n** (or Enter on it) opens a popover with every badge of
  the row, each followed by its reading in words, in place of a text-only
  tooltip. A badge there does what it does in the row: ratio stability opens
  its pairs, and a series, co-variation or batch-facts badge opens its rows.
