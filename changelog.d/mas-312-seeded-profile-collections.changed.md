- **The shipped charge-transfer chemistries are named for what they are.**
  The two shipped modes named *Ambient, negative* and *Ambient, positive*
  are now *Charge transfer (EASY-IC), negative* and *positive*, as the
  assignment profiles name them: read as an Orbitrap's, their electron
  transfer is the EASY-IC source, and "ambient" means an APi-TOF's own ions
  with no reagent. A start renames them in place under the same ids, unless
  another mode already holds the new name or acquisition batches are named
  for either.
