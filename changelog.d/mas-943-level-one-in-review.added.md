- **Level 1 is a person's statement, scoped and open to refutation.** A
  confirmation now says what its evidence showed, and the level it states
  carries the shared scale's letter. A reference standard on this instrument
  with an independent line beside it (MS/MS, a separation or another
  instrument) is level 1, and without one 2b. MS/MS is 2a, 2b, or for one
  loss 3b, a class named without a structure. Orthogonal evidence is 2b or 3a
  to 3d, and the isotope/adduct pattern 4a or 4b as the split of molecule and
  reagent is pinned or open. A level-1 verdict on a batch peak names the time
  window its standard covers, and a sample acquired outside it keeps the
  verdict on the formula but names no structure and reads the engine's
  level. Rejecting on a reference standard records the claim it
  refutes. The verify routes take `evidence_detail`, `independent_line` and,
  on a batch peak, `window_start_utc` and `window_end_utc`. Every verdict read
  returns `level_letter`, the SDK's `peak_assignments.verify` takes the two
  fields, and the assignment bench's review sheet takes two columns for them.
  Claims recorded before keep their level and read without a letter.
