- **A batch decision measured on a sample weighs the rivals a run would.**
  The batch search's propagation, *Detect series* and an imported batch
  result measured their reading on each sample's peak with no rival beside
  it, so the candidate-density rule (*rivals left standing*) never held it:
  on a crowded mass the sample the search read held the reading at
  *candidate* while every other sample read it *assigned*, and the batch
  peak's consensus followed them. Each measured reading now has the
  formulas the search's element ranges hold for its peak's mass scored
  beside it, as a run's search scores them, and those the evidence cannot
  separate count as its rivals. A reading a list holds also records what
  the search saw on its peak, so its identification level lists those
  rivals instead of reading them as not measured.
