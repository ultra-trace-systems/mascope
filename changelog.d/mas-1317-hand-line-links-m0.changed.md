- **A peak assigned by hand as an isotope line folds under its compound.** When the
  sample's run holds the main line of the same formula and adduct, the line is linked
  to it: it folds under that row in **Assignments** and shows its level, and keeps
  the tier its own evidence gives it. A main line assigned by hand later takes such
  lines too. When the run holds no such main line, or more than one, the line stands
  on its own, and the message says which case it was. Replacing the main line's
  compound no longer unassigns a line assigned by hand: it keeps its formula and
  stands on its own until the compound comes back.
