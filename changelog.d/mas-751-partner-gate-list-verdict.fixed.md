- **A secondary-channel reading a reference list names is no longer capped
  again when the partner it was swapped in on goes.** The partner gate can
  turn an opportunistic reading of an ion into another opportunistic reading
  whose molecule the sample shows. It recorded the second channel as that
  reading's corroboration even where a list names the molecule, so a later
  round of the gate that lost the partner held the row at *candidate*. It now
  records the list, as the minor-channel cap itself does, and a list does not
  go away (`docs/user/how-it-works/peak-assignment.md`, *minor channel only*).
