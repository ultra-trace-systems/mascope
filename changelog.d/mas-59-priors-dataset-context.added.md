- **A dataset or a batch names the chemistry context its samples were drawn
  from.** **Chemistry context** in the dialog that creates or edits a dataset
  or a batch, and `chemistry_context` on their routes, names a context such as
  ambient air or a chamber. A peak assignment run on *Auto* searches under the
  batch's, else the dataset's, else its reagent profile's own, and records
  which; the launcher and the run selector say where it came from.

- **Chlorine and bromine are read from their twin line.** A peak whose 37Cl or
  81Br line sits unexplained at the exact offset and one atom's ratio is
  searched again over carbon, hydrogen, oxygen and that halogen, and the
  reading replaces the peak's where it explains the line and passes the run's
  gates. A certified mixture's chlorobenzene, which no charge-transfer grid
  reaches, is read this way. The row names the twin and leads its alternatives
  with what it replaced.

- **Nitrate runs read an organonitrate's carbon skeleton.** On an Orbitrap, in
  a context that makes organonitrates, the context's windows and the oxygen
  lattice signature also judge a formula with up to three nitrate or nitro
  groups taken off, so dinitrates and nitroaromatics the windows refused are
  read. A row only that reading admits is held at *candidate* (*organonitrate
  skeleton*).
