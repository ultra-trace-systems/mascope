- **A Continuity column in the batch ledger.** Once a batch's ratio stability
  has been graded, the *Batch peaks* ledger shows each batch peak's grade
  after *Samples*: *stable*, *drift* and *red* as a badge, *inconclusive* and
  *insufficient* muted, and *Not graded* on a batch that is not time-ordered.
  A dashed badge is partial, some pairs left ungraded; a clock marks a grade
  the ledger has moved past since. Clicking it lists the pairs behind the
  grade, each with its ratio, spread and drift. An isotope line shows its own
  grade when the isotopologues are unfolded. The column sorts, red first, and
  moves no tier.
