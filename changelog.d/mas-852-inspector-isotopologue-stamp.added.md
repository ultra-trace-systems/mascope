- **The peak inspector shows the batch's isotopologue stamp.** A peak *Stamp
  isotopologues* stamped in a sample now says so in that sample's inspector, in one
  line under the channel badge: whose isotope line the batch reads it as, and whether
  the stamp was predicted or found by the peak picker. Click it to select the parent's
  peak. The parent's own row counts its stamped isotope lines and lists them on hover.
  The assignment detail route and the SDK's `peak_assignments.detail` carry both as
  `batch_stamped_isotopologue_of` and `batch_diagnostic_isotopologues`. Display-only:
  no tier moves.
