- **Assign batch: one control that assigns a batch and says where it stands.** At batch
  level the Assignments bar shows whether the batch holds known compositions only, is
  up to date, is behind and why (new samples, a changed library, changed settings, an
  older engine), is
  being assigned, or stopped at a stage, and offers the one action that fits: *Assign
  batch*, *Update assignment*, *Re-assign*, *Retry* or *Cancel*. Its menu folds every
  sample again (*Re-assign from scratch*), opens the run history and exports the
  ledger. A pass is one entry in the progress strip, and ends in place or with one
  *Batch assignment* toast. The empty batch ledger offers *Assign batch* too.
