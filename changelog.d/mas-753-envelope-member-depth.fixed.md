- **A small ion's M+1 peak on a TOF is no longer predicted a few percent low.**
  The predicted envelope now takes in isotope lines down to a thousandth of its floor
  when it adds up the lines one peak holds. A small ion's 2H and 17O lines fall under
  the old depth of a tenth of the floor, so they were left out of the 13C peak they sit
  in: by 2.6% for C4H8NO+. The untargeted search, the check that reads a peak as a
  neighbour's isotope line and the reagent clusters' isotopologue claims all read it
  (#3024).
