- **The shipped Uronium, positive chemistry calibrates files whose scan window
  starts above the urea series, and searches ammonium adducts.** Its calibrant
  collection carries, behind the protonated urea monomer, dimer and trimer, the
  cyclic siloxanes D3 to D8 and caprolactam, which a uronium source's
  background carries: a siloxane is fitted on as its protonated ion and its
  urea adduct, caprolactam as its urea adduct (m/z 174.124). A file is fitted
  on the ones it holds. On the demo dataset's 81 uronium files of a window from
  m/z 122, which hold no usable urea line, none calibrated under the shipped
  chemistry before. The chemistry also declares `[M+NH4]+`, which 27 % of the
  demo's uronium golden rows are. A server brings its existing Uronium,
  positive mode onto the new mechanisms at its next start, as a new version
  where files were processed under it, and those files keep their results
  until they are processed again. The same start brings the shipped
  calibrant collection up to date, so every batch holding Uronium, positive
  files is flagged *recalibrate* after the upgrade. Caprolactam ships as a reference list of its
  own, tagged `background` as the siloxanes are.
