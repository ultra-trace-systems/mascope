- **One mark, one meaning in the assignment views.** Each batch reading
  beside a formula in the *Batch peaks* ledger, and on the inspector's batch
  lines, is now a small square badge: its glyph says which reading it is, no
  two readings share one, and its colour says what it does to confidence -
  green supports, amber holds back, teal drifted, grey moves nothing, an
  outline is a person's change. The tier chips show a dot in place of an
  icon (filled, half, a ring, a dashed ring for *unassigned*), the reagent
  and artifact chips a purple that reads on both themes, and an unjudged row
  no verdict mark at all. A batch-level verdict that reaches a sample is its
  own icon with the batch's **#**, and the **Listed as** column folds to the
  name cut short rather than a flask. The tier help card has a legend of the
  marks.
