- **A refit worse than the confidence curve in force no longer replaces it.**
  The recalibration route kept the new curve whatever its held-out
  calibration error; it now keeps the curve in force and says why. Verdicts
  that make no usable curve are reported rather than failing the request,
  and a verdict on a formula-search row of a run before engine 0.5.0, scored
  on the older scale, no longer reaches the refit.
