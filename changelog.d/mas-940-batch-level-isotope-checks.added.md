- **A batch peak states an identification level of its own.** *Assign batch* ends with
  a new stage, *Identification level*, which reads the batch's isotope checks: a halogen
  lock, or a stamped 34S, 29Si or 30Si line, puts out the rivals that cannot make it
  (5a to 4), the label twin read present pins a labelled ion's split (4b to 4a), and a
  peak stamped as another peak's 37Cl or 81Br line under a reading holding no such
  halogen reads 5b. The *Batch peaks* ledger's **Level** column shows the batch's level,
  with the members' split in its hover, and the level chip's popover shows it first,
  with its evidence and lift, above the samples' own split; the peak inspector shows it
  beside a sample row's own level where the batch moves it; the batch data export
  carries it as `batch_level`, `batch_level_evidence` and `batch_level_lift`. No tier
  moves.
