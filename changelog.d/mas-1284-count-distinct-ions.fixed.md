- **A list hit's grid rivals are drawn from 64 ions, not 64 spellings.**
  When the assignment weighs a reference-list reading against the formula
  search's candidates for its peak, it scores the 64 nearest. Readings of
  one ion through different neutrals or mechanisms each took a place of
  those 64, so a dense window scored fewer distinct ions than the
  untargeted search does on the same peak, and its identification level
  counted the rest as left unscored. The window is now elected to one
  reading per ion first, as the untargeted search's is.
