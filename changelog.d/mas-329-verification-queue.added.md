- **A verification queue in the sample ledger.** The **to verify** chip at the
  end of the tier strip counts the assignments worth a verdict next and shows
  only them, in order: verdicts a later run read differently first, then rows
  near a tier edge where the instrument holds few verdicts, with random spot
  checks between them. A column says why each row is asked, and the queue
  shows no probability or evidence beside the row.
