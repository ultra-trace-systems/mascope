- **A list match no isotope line was read for stays a candidate.** A compound matched
  from the target library or a reference list used to reach *assigned* on its mass and
  the list alone, though no line of its isotope pattern could be read. It now stays a
  *candidate*, with the reason *no isotope line read*, unless a second ionization
  channel or a series of its batch corroborates it. This holds on every instrument. Its
  identification level does not change.
