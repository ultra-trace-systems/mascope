- **A row the batch reads as a compound's isotope line says why it has its
  tier.** *Strip isotopologue rows* rewrites a sample's row into the
  isotopologue of a brighter compound, at that compound's tier, and left
  the row with no tier reasons: the inspector's **Why** box was empty and
  the tier chip showed no cap. The row now carries the reason every
  isotopologue row does, that its tier is its compound's, marked as a cap
  where the compound's row is capped. Rows stripped before keep no
  reasons until the stage runs again.
