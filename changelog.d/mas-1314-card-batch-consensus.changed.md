- **The inspector card names the batch's consensus when the batch ledger opens it.**
  Opened from a *Batch peaks* row, the card's line under the chips now names the batch's
  formula, its tier and how many samples hold the peak with the share of their formulas
  that agree, then the sample shown and whether its own run agrees, reads another
  formula, reads none, or meets a consensus that is not settled. Before, a sample whose
  run read another formula headed the card with no word of what the batch settled on.
