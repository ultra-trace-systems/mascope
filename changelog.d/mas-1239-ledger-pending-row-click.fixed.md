- **A click on an Assignments row while the sample's peaks load is kept.**
  The Sample tab's ledger can list its rows before the sample's peak list
  is in, and a click on a row then did nothing: no selection, no focused
  peak, no inspector. The row now stays selected and its peak opens once
  the list lands for the same sample. A click on another row, a second
  click on the same one, or a switch to another sample before then drops
  it.
