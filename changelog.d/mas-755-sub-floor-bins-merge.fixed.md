- **Two faint isotope lines one peak holds together are no longer dropped from the predicted envelope.**
  Where a file's resolving power is known, two lines each under the envelope's floor,
  less than one FWHM apart and over the floor together, are now predicted as one line.
  Before, one of them could be added to a brighter line and the other dropped: the M+1
  peak of C6H12N2O2S+ was predicted 9% low at a resolving power of 40000 but not at
  35000. The untargeted search, the check that reads a peak as a neighbour's isotope line
  and the reagent clusters' isotopologue claims all read it (#3027).
