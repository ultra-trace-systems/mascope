- **A TOF's isotope lines are read at its own peak width.** The absent-line rule used to
  look for a predicted line within the 15 ppm match window, though a TOF peak is 110 to
  500 ppm wide, and asked for lines the peak list could never have kept. On the bench's
  TOF nitrate sets it refuted most readings, nearly always on a 13C line that was in the
  file. A line is now looked for within half the file's peak width, asked only where its
  predicted height reaches the smallest peak the list keeps near it, and read as *not
  tested* beside a taller peak within one peak width. Only an absent line caps a row and
  puts it at level 5b; a row whose line could not be read reads 5a, the line named.

- **A bromine reading's 81Br line is looked for whenever the peak list could show it.**
  The 81Br line is nearly as tall as the main one, but on an Orbitrap it was asked only
  where the run's detection threshold said a line should show. A faint bromide adduct
  therefore had no line of its pattern checked. The line is now asked wherever the peak
  list could hold it, on every instrument.
