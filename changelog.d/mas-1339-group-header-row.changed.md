- **Group by draws its group lines as headers.** A group line in the assignment ledger
  is now bold on a shaded ground of its own, its count in a badge, and no longer shows a
  tier: a group has no tier of its own. The count reads as the ledger's header does: in
  batch peaks in the batch scope, in peaks in a sample. Every row under it carries a bar
  on its left edge, so it is plain which rows a group holds.
