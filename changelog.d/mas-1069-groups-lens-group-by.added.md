- **See the rows a reading spans together.** In the batch ledger, a series,
  co-variation or batch facts badge, or the new `×N` pill beside an ion, now
  narrows the ledger to the group it is about. The rows come in the group's
  own order, a Step or Channel column labels each one, and a series step
  with no batch peak stays greyed in the list. A bar above the table names
  the group and offers **Plot the group**; its × or Escape gives the ledger
  back. In a sample's ledger the `×N` pill does the same from the batch's
  groups, listing the members the sample does not hold as *not in this
  sample*. The inspector's member lists gain **Show in the ledger** and
  **Plot them**. **Show › Group by** now draws collapsible group lines, by
  compound in both ledgers and by series in the batch's, with the rest under
  *Ungrouped*. It replaces the sample ledger's head row with its channels
  under it.
