- **The series may move a batch peak with isotope lines to another reading of its ion.**
  Since the series yielded to the isotope lines, a batch peak whose lines were
  stripped or stamped kept its reading against every series proposal. Its lines
  are predicted from its ion alone, though, so a proposal of the same ion through
  another ionization (C6H6O with formate and C7H8O3 deprotonated are both
  C7H7O3-) leaves nothing for the isotope lines to read again. The series now
  measures such a proposal as any other. If the peak moves, its isotope-line
  records and its lines' records stay and name the reading it holds now. A
  proposal of another ion still yields, and a completed *Assign batch* still
  leaves every stage current.
