- **Peak assignment predicts an Orbitrap's merged isotope line as the peak's
  height.** Isotope lines closer than half a peak width are one peak. On a
  TOF, whose intensity is a peak's area, that line is still the sum of its
  members. On an Orbitrap, whose intensity is a peak's height, it was summed
  the same way, so the line was predicted too tall and off its peak. Its peak
  then read low, and siloxane silicon lines fell out of Stage A to be read as
  compounds of their own. Such a line is now its brightest member, at that
  member's m/z, with only the members very close to it added. The farther
  members stay named on the line. Stage A and the untargeted search predict
  the same lines.
