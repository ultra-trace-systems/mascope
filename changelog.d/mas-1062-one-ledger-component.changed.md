- **The sample and batch ledgers read alike.** The *Assignments* ledger of a
  sample and the *Batch peaks* ledger of a batch are now one ledger in two
  scopes, with the same column names in sentence case and formulas in the
  monospaced face. An empty cell is blank in both, with no dash and no
  *unassigned*. An unfolded isotopologue line is named by its substitution,
  such as *[13C]* or *[81Br]*, wherever the run, the batch's stamp, its strip
  or the compound's record of its lines names it; *M+1* only where nothing
  does. The tier chips filter by several tiers at once in both ledgers, and
  the batch ledger's Tier column loses its filter menu. The Verdict column
  sorts in both. A ledger that fails to load says so in place of its table,
  with the tier chips still usable, and its count moves into its header
  line in place of its own breadcrumb.
