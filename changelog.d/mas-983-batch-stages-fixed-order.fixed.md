- **Ratio-stability grades no longer depend on the order of *Read
  co-variation* and the ratio pass.** The ratio stability runs after the
  co-variation in every pass, so a drifting channel pair whose channels
  co-vary reads `drift` whichever step was pressed first.

- **A later known-species decision that reads a species absent withdraws the
  earlier claims.** The members and in-app rows it rewrote read again what
  they read before, but for rows a person assigned by hand since; a water
  ladder rung that no longer passes withdraws its claims the same way.

- **A water-ladder claim stays the batch's after the ladder's record comes
  off.** The mark lives on the member, so *Decide known species* no longer
  counts such a member as its file's own test.

- **Detect series after an interrupted detection measures every sample.** A
  proposal on a batch peak that already reads its formula is measured on the
  members that do not read it yet, and a sample folded in while a detection
  runs no longer takes the series' records off.

- **A stamped isotope line pairs in the ratio stability with the row it is
  drawn under.** The batch ledger and the ratio pass read one owner link per
  isotope line (`line_owner_id` on the ledger rows), so the Continuity badge
  describes the pair the row is shown in.
