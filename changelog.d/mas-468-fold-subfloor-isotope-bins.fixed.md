- **A faint isotope line one peak cannot separate is no longer dropped from the predicted envelope.**
  Where a file's resolving power is known, the untargeted search, the check that reads a
  peak as a neighbour's isotope line and the reagent clusters' isotopologue claims add a
  line under the envelope's floor to a kept line less than one FWHM away, instead of
  dropping it. The 15N line beside a 13C line was lost at some resolving powers, so the
  M+1 peak was predicted up to 10% low, and lower on a file of higher resolving power
  than on one of lower (#2591).
