- **A faint isotope line one peak cannot separate is no longer dropped from the predicted envelope.**
  Where a file's resolving power is known, the untargeted search adds a line under the
  envelope's floor to a kept line less than one FWHM away, instead of dropping it. The
  15N line beside a 13C line was lost at some resolving powers, so the M+1 peak was
  predicted up to 10% low, and lower on a file of higher resolving power than on one
  of lower (#2591).
