- **The inspector card says why an isotope line was not asked.** The Isotope
  pattern section notes the share of M0 the run asked lines from at this peak,
  and that the lines under it were not asked. Each line's hover gives its
  outcome and the numbers it was asked at. A row with only M0 says why
  nothing was asked, and the isotope tile counts the lines found of those
  asked.
