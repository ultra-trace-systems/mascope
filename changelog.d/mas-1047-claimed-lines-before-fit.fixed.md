- **A batch decision the sample's lists hold is fitted as a run fits it.**
  When a batch stage measures a reading on a sample whose lists hold its ion,
  its lines on peaks the reagent or artifact pass claimed are left out before
  the fit, as a run's Stage A leaves them out. Before, such a line still
  counted, as found or as missing, so the reading's fit and tier could differ
  from the run's row.
