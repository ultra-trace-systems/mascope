- **A possible in-source fragment is named among a row's reasons.** Where a run
  commits a neutral and, through the same channel and on a shorter peak, that
  neutral less a small neutral an ion sheds in the source (water, carbon
  monoxide or dioxide, formic acid, methanol, nitric acid, sulfur trioxide),
  the lighter row's **Why this tier** names the heavier one, the loss and the
  two peaks' height ratio, marked as a grouping that caps nothing. A
  neutral seen through two channels or more also has, on its brightest row
  of each channel, that channel's ratio to each of its other channels, read
  against the same ratio over five or more of the sample's other neutrals
  (`cross_channel.intensity_consistency`, served by the API and the SDK).
  Neither reading moves a tier or P(correct); both were measured first,
  and the measurement is in the assignment plan's step 4.16 (tiering rule
  set 14).
