- **A batch series no longer lifts a faint lone peak to assigned.** A
  homologous series of the batch still counts as a second observation of a
  formula found on one peak, but only on a peak with a signal-to-noise of 4
  or more. Under 4 the row stays a candidate, and its reason names the
  series, the floor and the peak's signal-to-noise. A row with no
  signal-to-noise recorded is lifted as before. The tiering rules are now
  version 16.
