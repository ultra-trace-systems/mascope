- **A main line assigned by hand claims its isotope lines.** When you assign a
  peak as a compound's main line, the isotope lines the spectrum shows for it
  on peaks the run left unassigned become lines under it, as a run writes
  them: they fold under it in **Assignments**, at no higher a tier and with
  its level. A line is claimed only as a run's match would take it, by its
  mass error and its height. A line on a peak another row holds, or one you
  or an earlier change left unassigned, is left as it is. The message lists
  the lines claimed and the ones left, with what each peak reads now.
  Replacing the main line unassigns its claimed lines with it, and the undo
  puts them back. *Use* and **Assign** both do this.
