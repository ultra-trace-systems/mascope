- **A sample's next run keeps a peak its batch kept.** A run claims the source
  ions its batch decided are there, a known species or a water-ladder rung,
  after the target library and the search have read the sample, not before
  them. It then keeps their reading where the batch's own claim keeps a
  sample's: the target library names the peak (or, for a rung, a reference
  list), the peak is another reading's isotope line or another claim's, or the
  reading carries an isotope line the ion cannot have. Before, the run claimed
  such a peak for the source and its fold brought a reagent member into the
  batch peak, so the sample's ledger and the batch's disagreed until the next
  pass. A claimed ion's row keeps the reading it displaced among its
  alternatives, and a decoy run claims what its control claimed.
