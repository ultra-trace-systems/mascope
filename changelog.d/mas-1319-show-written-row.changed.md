- **Assignments shows the row a hand assignment wrote.** After **Use** on a
  close alternative or the hand button of the formula search, the ledger opens
  the family the row folds into, selects the row and scrolls to it, without
  shutting a family you had open. An isotope line folded under its main line
  carries the hand mark when a hand edit wrote it, and its hover says whether
  a person assigned it or the hand assignment of its main line claimed it.
  When a tier chip, the search, the **to verify** chip, the lens or a choice
  under **Show** hides the row, a note under the filter bar names that filter.
  The ledger's rows carry `manual_action`, the hand edit that wrote a row.
