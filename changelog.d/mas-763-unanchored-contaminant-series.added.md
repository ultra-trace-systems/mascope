- **Detect series finds a siloxane or CF2 ladder nothing anchors.** The
  siloxane and CF2 steps start only from a batch peak assigned a formula
  carrying silicon or fluorine, so a ladder of column bleed or of
  perfluorinated acids that the batch does not assign went unfound. *Detect
  series* now also links those two units by mass alone among the batch peaks
  nothing assigns, or holds only at candidate, against the same decoys and
  on the same terms (12 links, ten times the decoys' mean). It does so in an
  ionization mode where nothing is assigned too. A kept ladder proposes no
  formula and marks no row: the run's summary lists its batch peaks and
  their m/z per mode, with its links against the decoys, and the run's
  message names it.
