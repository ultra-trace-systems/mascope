- **Detect series finds a siloxane or CF2 ladder nothing anchors.** The
  siloxane and CF2 steps start only from a batch peak assigned a formula
  carrying silicon or fluorine, so a ladder of column bleed or of
  perfluorinated acids that the batch does not assign went unfound. *Detect
  series* now also links those two units by mass alone among the batch peaks
  nothing assigns, or holds only at candidate or below assignability, against
  the same decoys and on the same terms (12 links, ten times the decoys'
  mean). A peak one isotope spacing above a brighter one is left out, so a
  ladder's own isotope lines do not count as further ladders. It does so in
  an ionization mode where nothing is assigned too. A kept ladder proposes no
  formula and marks no row: the run's summary lists its batch peaks and their
  m/z per mode, with its links against the decoys, and the run's message
  names it.
