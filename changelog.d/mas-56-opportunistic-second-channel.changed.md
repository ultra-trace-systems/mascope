- **A formula read through a secondary channel needs its molecule named
  elsewhere to reach assigned.** The formula search's reading through a
  channel the ionization mode treats as secondary, such as carbonate on a
  nitrate source or ammonium on a uronium one, is held at *candidate* unless a
  loaded reference list or the target library names the molecule, or the
  sample commits it through one of the mode's own channels. Its own isotope
  lines no longer lift that hold: a 13C line gives the ion's carbon count, not
  which molecule clustered, and benzene and methanol read through carbonate
  reached *assigned* on that line alone. The lines still count toward the
  row's evidence. A list's own reading through a channel the run opened keeps
  its rule. The tiering rule set is 11.
