- **A sample's peak assignment no longer works out the isotope lines of
  reference-list compounds that have no peak near them.** With the shipped
  reference lists loaded, Stage A predicted the lines of every list ion at the
  file, about 30,000 lines a demo sample, though about four in five of the ions
  have no peak within the match window of any line. It now sets those aside
  first, and the arrival's fold and its neighbours' reads run faster. The
  assignments are the same.
