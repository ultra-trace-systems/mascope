- **A way back from a sample's assignments to the batch's.** A sample's
  Assignments ledger has a **Batch peaks** button in its header line, once the
  batch has batch peaks. It returns to the batch's ledger with its filters,
  sort and selection as they were, and when the sample was opened from a batch
  peak's row, that row comes back selected and scrolled into view. The step is
  one of the browser's history, so its Back and Forward buttons move between
  the two ledgers too.
