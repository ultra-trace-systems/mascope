  ([10.5281/zenodo.23984343](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23984343)),
  the raw files and reference data of 1.4.3 rebuilt with this release's
  isotope matching: an Orbitrap isotope line's ratio is read over the scans
  it shares with its ion's most abundant line. 607 dim isotope lines now
  read outside the isotope ratio tolerance and 27 lines score anew, for
  42,424 golden peaks against 43,004; every golden peak the two bundles
  share keeps its m/z and height. 56 ions change category and one is gone,
  for 17,394 against 17,395. The MS2 spectra keep their m/z and intensity.
  1.4.3 stays registered so a pinned run still resolves.
