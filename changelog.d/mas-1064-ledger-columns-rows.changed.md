- **The assignment ledgers show one column set per scope, and quieter rows.**
  A sample's **Assignments** ledger and the batch's **Batch peaks** ledger now
  share the columns Plot, m/z, Compound, Ion, Intensity, Tier and Level, and
  the batch's adds Samples (as *n/N*) and **Across the batch**. Compound
  writes what a list or a target names the formula under it, in place of the
  *Listed as* column. Ion is the mechanism, with a **×N** pill where the
  sample reads the neutral through N channels, or the batch peak's ion.
  Intensities read as compact numbers (`8.31k`, `1.20M`) beside a log-scale
  bar. The batch's readings, whose co-variation, facts and continuity columns
  are gone, are at most three badges in Across the batch, the ones that hold
  the reading back first, then `+n`. A rule that holds a tier down is a mark
  on its chip, and an imported engine's tier and level are a second, outlined
  chip behind **Show › Engine tier** rather than columns. Both ledgers open
  sorted by intensity, brightest first. A caret with the line count unfolds
  one isotopologue family, and its lines are sub-rows with their label, m/z
  and intensity only. Clicking an m/z opens the peak in the inspector, in the
  batch's brightest sample there. A row's action (Curate, or the brightest
  sample) shows on hover, on focus and on the selected row. A narrow ledger
  drops the level, the samples, the badges and the bar, in that order.
