- **A confirmation can name the structure it identifies.** The verdict forms in
  the peak inspector and in the assignment and batch peak ledgers offer the
  compounds the row's reference lists name, or an InChIKey of your own marked
  *Unlisted*. The confirmation then states an identification level after
  Schymanski et al. 2014: 1 on a reference standard, 2 on MS/MS, 3 on
  orthogonal evidence, 4 on the isotope/adduct pattern, fixed when it is
  recorded. The verdict badge shows the level and the structure. The verify
  routes take `claimed_inchikey` and `claimed_name`, every verdict read returns
  them with `level`, the SDK's `peak_assignments.verify` takes them, and the
  *Verifications* sheet of the batch spreadsheet carries the three columns.
  Verdicts recorded before read as level 4 with no structure.
