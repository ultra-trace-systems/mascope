- **The Level column and the inspector's level chip read a live claim's level.** Where a
  verdict states a level, such as *Confirmed · Level 2b* on a named structure, the
  sample and batch ledgers' **Level** column, its filter and the inspector's chip now
  show that level in place of the engine's, and the chip's hover says whose it is,
  *claim* or *engine*. A level-1 batch claim reaches only the samples inside its window,
  and a claim recorded before the scale's letters shows its number alone.
