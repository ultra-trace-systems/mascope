- **Peak assignment scores a low-mass line at the width its mass z reads.** Where a
  run's centre follows the mass range, a row's *mass z* was read in a width floored at
  0.03 mDa below the masses the centre was fitted on, and its fit score in the run's one
  width. Both now read the floored width, line by line. On an Orbitrap this reaches only
  lines below about m/z 50.
