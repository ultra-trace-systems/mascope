- **Isomeric targets in one batch peak each keep their compound.** When two samples'
  peaks at one batch peak were matched to two isomeric target compounds through the same
  ion, such as glucose and fructose, the batch kept only the first. A sample read off the
  batch ledger showed that first compound on its rows, and the *Batch peaks* ledger named
  it for the whole peak. Each member now keeps the compound its own sample matched. A
  sample's rows show their own target, and the batch peak names a target only where its
  members agree. A batch folded before this change shows each sample's own target once
  that sample is folded again.
