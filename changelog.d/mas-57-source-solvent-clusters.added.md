- **The reagent pass claims the clusters a positive source builds of its
  solvent vapour.** On the charge-transfer and urea sources, the clusters of
  methanol, ethanol and acetone with a proton or a hydronium, and ethanol and
  acetone less a hydride holding a second molecule, are claimed as reagent
  rows on their ladder: the solvent's own ion is in the spectrum, so is every
  rung below, each step is one molecule's exact mass, and the heights fall. A
  rung whose ion also reads as a molecule takes the peak only where its
  ladder outweighs what the spectrum shows of that molecule through another
  channel, and a rung holding water never does. On a nitrate source the acid
  dimer core's first three hydrates are claimed the same way. On a chamber
  dataset's positive charge-transfer batch, the acetone dimer that read as a
  C6H12O2 analyte in three of six samples is now the source's ion
  (`docs/user/how-it-works/peak-assignment.md`, *Ladders of the source's
  own*).
